# MERCYMAP demo video script (target 4:30, limit 5:00)

Record on https://mercymap.vercel.app in a clean browser window at 1280×800
or larger, zoom 110 to 125 percent, with notifications off. Use the fictional
demo church Hope Harvest Chapel. Speak slowly; the timings include pauses.

Judging weights to hit: Ministry Impact 30%, Execution & Quality 25%,
Innovation 20%, Faithfulness & Responsible AI 15%, Presentation 10%.

## Before recording

- Sign in as the demo **owner** (theretiredblues@gmail.com) in one window.
- Have an InPrivate window open on the home page.
- Prepare the fictional request text (below) to paste.
- On request MM-E4373B-066893 (welfare concern), leave the flag open so you
  can clear it on camera, or clear it beforehand if you skip that scene.

## 1. Why (0:00 to 0:30). Home page

> "Most churches already help people: food, school fees, hospital bills. But
> the help runs on WhatsApp messages and memory. Requests get lost, stock runs
> out after it has been promised, and the most sensitive cases depend on who
> happened to read the message. MERCYMAP gives every church one trusted place
> for every request, from the first message to the help delivered."

Scroll slowly to the Matthew 25:40 verse.

> "It is built on Matthew 25:40: whatever we do for the least of these, we do
> for Christ."

## 2. Asking for help, no account (0:30 to 1:05). InPrivate window

Open `mercymap.vercel.app/request/hope-harvest-chapel`.

> "Each church shares its own link. No app, no account. A mother can ask for
> help in plain words."

Fill in: Food and groceries; "My husband lost his job last month. We are
five at home and the food has run out."; quantity 1; contact Call me with
`0800 000 3001`; area Oke-Aro; tick consent; show "Success!" from Cloudflare;
send.

> "She gets a reference number. The form is clear that it is not an emergency
> service, and it tells her how the church will use what she wrote."

## 3. AI that assists, people who decide (1:05 to 2:05). Owner window

Open Requests, then the new request at the top.

> "Claude has already read it. Staff get a short summary, the key facts, what
> to ask next, and an urgency level."

Point to the line "Check before acting. The AI suggests; people decide."

> "Her name and phone number were removed before anything reached the AI. And
> the AI cannot approve, decline or contact anyone. Only staff can."

Open the student nurse request (Awaiting match).

> "When a request is ready, the AI looks at the church's own stock and
> explains which resource fits and why. Here it suggests the family food
> package."

## 4. Safeguarding (2:05 to 2:40)

Open the teenage-girl request (Needs attention).

> "Some requests are not just about food. Here a girl is not safe at home.
> The keyword check flagged it and the AI rated it high urgency. MERCYMAP
> never decides this on its own. The flag stays until a person reviews it,
> following the church's own safeguarding procedure, and records what was
> done."

(Optional: on MM-E4373B-066893, write "Added to the weekly visiting list" and
click Mark as reviewed: "and the review is recorded with their name and the
time.")

## 5. Honest stock and volunteers (2:40 to 3:30)

Go back to the student nurse request. In "Approve and reserve", choose the
Family food package and approve.

> "Approving reserves the stock in the same step, so two people can never be
> promised the last package."

Show Resources briefly (available and reserved numbers).

Update status to In progress, then Give task to Tunde Adeyemi.

> "A volunteer gets a minimal brief, by WhatsApp or copy. Only what they need
> to deliver, nothing more."

Click Mark as fulfilled.

> "Every step is in the request's history and the audit log."

## 6. Impact for leaders (3:30 to 4:05)

Open Overview, then Reports & Impact, then Last 365 days.

> "Leaders see what needs attention today, what is running low, and over the
> year how many people the church served, how fast, and with what. Exports
> leave out names and phone numbers."

## 7. Built for many churches (4:05 to 4:30)

Back to the home page or Overview.

> "MERCYMAP is multi-church from day one: each church's data is kept apart in
> the database itself, and a church only goes live after it is verified. It
> is built in Nigeria for African churches first, and it works for any
> congregation that wants to love its neighbours well. MERCYMAP: turning
> church compassion into coordinated community impact."
